About MISTER

FAQ

What happens after I submit a report?

The report is placed in Pending status until it is reviewed.

Who decides what happens to a report?

The owner reviews the report and decides whether it should be approved, rejected, or escalated.

Is every report automatically accepted?

No. Submitting a report does not automatically trigger an action.
