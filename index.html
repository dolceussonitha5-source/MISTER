MISTER / ABOUT

About MISTER

MISTER is a report management interface designed to organize submitted reports and provide an owner-controlled review workflow.

How it works

A submitted report starts as Pending. The Owner can review the information and choose whether to Approve, Reject or Escalate the report.

MISTER / FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What happens after I submit a report?

The report is added to the review queue with Pending status.

Does submitting a report automatically take action?

No. A report is reviewed before an action is selected.

Who decides what happens to a report?

The authorized Owner reviews the report and selects Approve, Reject or Escalate.

Can I track my reports?

Yes. The Dashboard shows report history and activity statistics.
